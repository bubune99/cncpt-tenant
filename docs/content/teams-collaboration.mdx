# Teams & Collaboration

Work together on sites with your team. Invite members, assign roles, and control who can edit what.

## Creating a Team

1. Go to **Dashboard > Teams**.
2. Click **Create Team**.
3. Give your team a name (e.g., "Marketing Team" or "Client: Acme Corp").
4. You are automatically the team owner.

## Inviting Members

From the team page, click **Invite Member** and enter their email address. They will receive an invitation link to join.

- Invitations expire after 7 days.
- You can resend or cancel pending invitations.
- Members need a CNCPT Web account to accept the invitation.

<Callout type="info">
Team members do not need their own subscription. They inherit access from the team owner's plan.
</Callout>

## Roles and Permissions

Each team member has a role that controls their access level:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Owner** | Full access. Manage billing, team members, and all sites. |
| **Admin** | Manage sites and team members. Cannot change billing. |
| **Editor** | Edit site content and pages. Cannot change site settings. |
| **Viewer** | View-only access to sites and analytics. |

## Assigning Sites to Teams

You can share individual sites with a team:

1. Open the site from your dashboard.
2. Go to **Site Settings > Team Access**.
3. Select the team and permission level.

This lets you give different teams access to different sites without sharing everything.

## Team Limits

The number of team members depends on your plan:

- **Starter** — 2 seats included.
- **Growth** — 3 seats included.
- **Pro** — 6 seats included.
- **Business** — 25 seats included.

Additional seats can be purchased as add-ons. See [Billing & Plans](/docs/billing-plans).

<Callout type="warning">
Removing a team member revokes their access immediately. Any unsaved work in their editor session will be lost.
</Callout>

## Activity Log

The team activity log shows recent actions by all members:

- Page edits and publishes
- Site settings changes
- Team membership changes
- Domain configuration updates

Access the activity log from **Teams > [Team Name] > Activity**.
