# Branding & White Label

Make your site truly yours. CNCPT Web supports full white-label branding so your visitors never see our platform.

## Brand Settings

Navigate to **Dashboard > Settings > Branding** to configure:

- **Logo** — Upload your logo for the site header. Supports PNG, SVG, and WebP formats.
- **Logo (Dark Mode)** — Optional separate logo for dark backgrounds.
- **Favicon** — The small icon shown in browser tabs. Upload a 32x32 or 64x64 PNG, or an SVG.
- **Brand Colors** — Primary and secondary colors used throughout your site.

<Callout type="tip">
Upload an SVG favicon for the sharpest display across all devices and resolutions.
</Callout>

## Color Customization

Set your brand colors and they will be applied across your site:

- **Primary Color** — Used for buttons, links, and interactive elements.
- **Secondary Color** — Used for accents and highlights.
- **Background** — Default page background color.
- **Text** — Default text color.

Colors are applied as CSS custom properties, so they work seamlessly with both light and dark mode.

## Theme Settings

Choose between:

- **Light** — Light background with dark text.
- **Dark** — Dark background with light text.
- **System** — Follows the visitor's operating system preference.

## White Label Features

On Pro and Business plans, you get full white-label capabilities:

- No CNCPT Web branding anywhere on your published site.
- Custom meta tags and Open Graph data.
- Custom manifest.json for PWA support.
- Branded email notifications (if email features are enabled).

<Callout type="info">
White-label branding only affects the published site. The editor and dashboard always show CNCPT Web branding.
</Callout>

## Importing Brand Assets

If you already have a brand kit, you can upload assets in bulk:

1. Go to **Branding > Import**.
2. Upload your logo files, favicon, and color palette.
3. Preview the result before saving.

## Best Practices

- Use SVG logos when possible for crisp rendering at any size.
- Test your color choices for accessibility — ensure sufficient contrast between text and background colors.
- Keep your favicon simple. Complex images do not render well at small sizes.
- Preview your site in both light and dark modes after making branding changes.
