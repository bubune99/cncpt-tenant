# Custom Domains

Every site starts with a `yoursite.cncptweb.com` subdomain. You can connect your own domain for a professional look.

## Adding a Custom Domain

1. Go to **Dashboard > Domains**.
2. Click **Add Domain**.
3. Enter your domain (e.g., `www.example.com` or `example.com`).
4. Select which site to connect it to.
5. Follow the DNS instructions shown on screen.

## DNS Configuration

You will need to add DNS records at your domain registrar (e.g., Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare).

### For root domains (`example.com`)

Add an **A record** pointing to our IP address:

```
Type: A
Host: @
Value: 76.76.21.21
```

### For subdomains (`www.example.com`)

Add a **CNAME record** pointing to your site:

```
Type: CNAME
Host: www
Value: cname.cncptweb.com
```

<Callout type="info">
DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to propagate, though most complete within 1-2 hours.
</Callout>

## SSL Certificates

SSL certificates are provisioned automatically once your DNS is verified. Your site will be accessible via HTTPS with no additional configuration required.

## Verification Status

Your domain will show one of these statuses:

- **Pending** — DNS records not yet detected. Wait for propagation or double-check your records.
- **Verified** — DNS is correct and SSL is being provisioned.
- **Active** — Domain is fully connected and serving your site.
- **Error** — Something went wrong. Check the error message for details.

## Troubleshooting

**Domain stuck on Pending?**
- Verify your DNS records match exactly what is shown in the dashboard.
- Check for conflicting records (e.g., an existing A record that needs to be removed).
- Try using a DNS checker tool to confirm propagation.

**SSL not working?**
- SSL provisioning can take up to 10 minutes after DNS verification.
- Ensure there is no CAA record blocking certificate issuance.

<Callout type="tip">
Use the AI chat assistant to troubleshoot domain issues. It can check your DNS records and suggest fixes.
</Callout>

## Removing a Domain

Go to **Dashboard > Domains**, find the domain, and click **Remove**. The site will continue to be accessible at its original subdomain.
