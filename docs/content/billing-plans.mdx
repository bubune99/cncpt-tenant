# Billing & Plans

CNCPT Web offers four plans to fit projects of every size. All plans include a free trial.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Pro | Business |
|---------|---------|--------|-----|----------|
| Price | $25/mo | $49/mo | $99/mo | $349/mo |
| Team seats | 2 | 3 | 6 | 25 |
| Bandwidth | 25GB/mo | 100GB/mo | 300GB/mo | 1TB/mo |
| Site storage | 1GB | 5GB | 30GB | 300GB |
| Media storage | 3GB | 15GB | 75GB | 750GB |
| AI credits | 300/mo | 1,500/mo | 7,500/mo | 75,000/mo |
| Custom domain | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Email | Email | Priority | Dedicated |

<Callout type="tip">
Save 17% by choosing annual billing. Annual plans are billed once per year.
</Callout>

## Free Trial

All plans include a free trial period:

- **Starter/Growth** — 7-day trial
- **Pro/Business** — 14-day trial

No credit card required to start. You will be prompted to add payment details before the trial ends.

## Add-Ons

Need more of a specific resource? Add-ons are available on all plans:

- **Extra Team Seat** — $15/month ($12/month on Business)
- **Extra Bandwidth** — $15 per 25GB (one-time)
- **Extra Site Storage** — $3/GB/month
- **Extra Media Storage** — $1/GB/month

## Managing Your Subscription

Go to **Dashboard > Billing** to:

- View your current plan and usage.
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan.
- Purchase add-ons or AI credit packs.
- Update payment methods.
- Download invoices.

### Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle.

### Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Make sure your usage fits within the new plan's limits before downgrading.

<Callout type="warning">
If your usage exceeds the new plan's limits after downgrading, you will need to remove resources (sites, team members, etc.) or stay on your current plan.
</Callout>

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing page. Your sites remain accessible until the end of your paid period. After that, they revert to the free plan limits.

## Payment Methods

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Invoices are sent to your account email after each payment.
