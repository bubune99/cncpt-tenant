# Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your command center. Everything you manage — sites, domains, teams, billing — lives here.

## Main Navigation

The sidebar gives you quick access to every section:

- **Sites** — List of all your sites with status indicators and quick actions.
- **Domains** — Manage custom domains and DNS verification.
- **Teams** — Invite collaborators and manage permissions.
- **Billing** — View your plan, usage, and invoices.
- **Settings** — Account preferences, branding, and security.

## Sites Overview

Your sites appear as cards showing:

- **Name and subdomain** — Click to open the site editor.
- **Status badge** — Published, Draft, or Maintenance.
- **Last edited** — Timestamp of the most recent change.
- **Quick actions** — Edit, preview, duplicate, or delete.

<Callout type="info">
Sites in Draft mode are only visible to you and your team. Publish when you are ready to go live.
</Callout>

## Analytics Snapshot

The dashboard home shows a quick analytics summary for all your sites:

- Total page views (last 30 days)
- Unique visitors
- Top-performing pages
- AI credit usage

For detailed analytics, open a specific site and navigate to its Analytics tab.

## AI Chat Assistant

The chat icon in the bottom-right corner opens the AI assistant. It can help you:

- Navigate the dashboard
- Answer questions about features
- Troubleshoot domain issues
- Explain your billing and usage

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl+K` | Open command palette |
| `Ctrl+N` | Create new site |
| `Ctrl+/` | Toggle AI chat |
