# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Getting Started

### How do I create an account?

Visit [cncptweb.com/register](/register) and sign up with your email. No credit card required. You will receive a verification email to confirm your account.

### Can I try CNCPT Web for free?

Yes. All plans include a free trial (7-14 days depending on the plan). You get full access to all features during the trial.

### What happens when my trial ends?

You will be prompted to select a plan and add payment details. If you do not subscribe, your sites become read-only. Your data is preserved for 30 days.

## Sites & Editing

### How many sites can I create?

It depends on your plan. Starter includes 1 site, Growth includes 3, Pro includes 10, and Business is unlimited. See [Billing & Plans](/docs/billing-plans) for details.

### Can I export my site?

Yes. The visual editor supports exporting your pages as React, Next.js, or Hydrogen code. Go to **Editor > Export** to download your site as a code project.

### Does CNCPT Web support e-commerce?

Yes. The platform includes built-in commerce features: product listings, cart, checkout, and payment processing via Stripe. You can also connect to Shopify as a headless commerce provider.

<Callout type="info">
E-commerce features are available as a module that can be enabled per site. No extra cost.
</Callout>

## Domains

### How long does DNS verification take?

Most DNS changes propagate within 1-2 hours, though it can take up to 48 hours in some cases. Check the Domains page in your dashboard for real-time status.

### Can I use a domain I already own?

Absolutely. Add it in **Dashboard > Domains** and follow the DNS instructions. We support root domains, subdomains, and wildcard domains.

## Teams

### Do team members need their own subscription?

No. Team members access your sites through your subscription. Each plan includes a set number of seats with the option to purchase additional ones.

### Can I transfer site ownership?

Not directly through the UI yet. Contact support and we can help with ownership transfers.

## Billing

### What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. We also support Apple Pay and Google Pay where available.

### Can I get a refund?

We offer a full refund within the first 14 days of a paid subscription. After that, you can cancel at any time and retain access until the end of your billing period. Contact support for refund requests.

### Do unused AI credits expire?

Monthly credits roll over up to 2x your allocation. Purchased credit packs never expire.

## Troubleshooting

### My site is not loading after publishing.

- Check the site status in your dashboard (should be "Published").
- If using a custom domain, verify DNS records are correct.
- Clear your browser cache and try an incognito window.
- Check the [status page](https://status.cncptweb.com) for any ongoing incidents.

### The editor is slow or unresponsive.

- Try refreshing the page.
- Close other browser tabs to free up memory.
- The editor works best in Chrome or Firefox with at least 4GB of available RAM.
- If the issue persists, contact support with your browser version and a screenshot.
