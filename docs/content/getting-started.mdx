# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to CNCPT Web. This guide walks you through creating your first site and publishing it live.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Sign up at [cncptweb.com/register](/register). No credit card is required for the free trial. You will be asked to verify your email address before proceeding.

## Step 2: Create a Site

After logging in, you will land on your dashboard. Click **Create New Site** and choose a template or start from scratch.

- **Template**: Pick a pre-built design from our marketplace. Templates cover e-commerce, portfolios, blogs, restaurants, and more.
- **Blank**: Start with an empty canvas and build your site block by block.

Give your site a name and pick a subdomain (e.g., `mysite.cncptweb.com`). You can connect a custom domain later.

## Step 3: Edit Your Site

Open the visual editor by clicking **Edit** on your site card. The editor has four main areas:

- **Block Palette** (left) — Drag blocks onto your page: headings, images, sections, buttons, and more.
- **Canvas** (center) — A live preview of your page. Click any block to select and edit it.
- **Properties Panel** (right) — Adjust styling, text, links, and animations for the selected block.
- **Outline** (top-right) — View and reorder your page structure as a tree.

<Callout type="tip">
Use the AI assistant in the bottom-right corner to generate content, suggest layouts, or answer questions about the editor.
</Callout>

## Step 4: Preview and Publish

Click **Preview** to see your site as visitors will. When you are satisfied, click **Publish**. Your site goes live immediately at your subdomain.

## Step 5: Connect a Domain (Optional)

Go to **Settings > Domains** in your dashboard to connect a custom domain. See the [Custom Domains](/docs/custom-domains) guide for DNS setup instructions.
