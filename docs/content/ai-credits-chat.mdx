# AI Credits & Chat

CNCPT Web includes an AI assistant powered by leading language models. It helps you build faster, write better content, and troubleshoot issues.

## What the AI Can Do

The AI assistant is available in two places:

### Dashboard Chat
Open the chat from the bottom-right corner of your dashboard. It can:

- Answer questions about your account, billing, and features.
- Navigate you to the right page or setting.
- Troubleshoot domain and configuration issues.
- Explain platform features and suggest next steps.

### Editor Chat
Inside the visual editor, the AI can:

- Generate page layouts and content.
- Suggest design improvements.
- Write copy for headings, paragraphs, and buttons.
- Help with responsive design and styling.

<Callout type="tip">
Be specific in your prompts. Instead of "make it look better", try "add a gradient background to this hero section with a centered heading."
</Callout>

## How Credits Work

Every AI interaction costs credits. The cost depends on the complexity of the request:

| Action | Approximate Cost |
|--------|-----------------|
| Simple question | 1 credit |
| Content generation | 2-5 credits |
| Layout suggestion | 3-8 credits |
| Image-related tasks | 5-15 credits |

## Monthly Allowance

Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance:

- **Starter** — 300 credits/month
- **Growth** — 1,500 credits/month
- **Pro** — 7,500 credits/month
- **Business** — 75,000 credits/month

Unused credits roll over up to 2x your monthly allocation.

## Purchasing More Credits

If you run out of credits, you can purchase additional packs:

- **Starter Pack** — 500 credits for $5
- **Growth Pack** — 2,200 credits for $15
- **Pro Pack** — 5,750 credits for $35
- **Enterprise Pack** — 18,000 credits for $99

Purchased credits never expire.

<Callout type="info">
Credit packs are available from **Dashboard > Billing > AI Credits**. Purchased credits are used after your monthly allowance.
</Callout>

## Checking Your Balance

Your current credit balance is always visible in the dashboard header. Click it to see a breakdown of:

- Monthly allowance remaining
- Rolled-over credits
- Purchased credits
- Usage history (last 30 days)
